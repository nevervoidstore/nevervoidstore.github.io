Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order. We never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, email, delivery address and the products and phone model you choose.
  • Photos you send us: for custom pet portraits.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram or email.

We don't store your card or UPI details. Payments are handled by Razorpay, our payment partner.

Cookies and ads

We use the Meta Pixel to understand which of our Facebook and Instagram ads lead people to our site and to orders. It records pages viewed and actions such as starting checkout, and Meta may use cookies to do this. It does not receive your address or payment details. You can manage ad settings in your Facebook or Instagram account.

How we use it

  • To make, ship and support your order.
  • To send you order updates and tracking details.
  • To answer your questions.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed with the partners who help us fulfil your order:

  • Our payment partner (Razorpay), to process your payment securely.
  • Our print and fulfilment partner in India, to print and pack your order.
  • Courier partners, to deliver it.

Your pet photos

Photos you send are used only to create your order. We will never post them or your portrait publicly without your permission.

This website

This website doesn't use advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record standard technical information such as your IP address.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time. We'll respond within 7 business days, except where we must keep records by law (for example, invoices).
