Christmas Gifts for Coworkers Under ₹700 That They'll Actually Keep

4 November 2026 · 2 min read

Office gifting has a drawer problem. Scented candles, novelty pens and generic chocolate boxes are pleasant for a minute, then forgotten. The gifts people keep share three things: they're useful every day, they feel a little personal, and they're easy to say yes to on a budget.

Here's a simple way to choose, with a few ideas from our own shelf.

1. Set the budget before you browse

Agree on a number with your team first, especially for Secret Santa. A clear cap (say ₹500 or ₹700) stops one person overspending and everyone else feeling awkward. It also makes choosing faster: you're comparing five options, not fifty.

2. Pick for the person, not the occasion

Instead of asking "what's a good Christmas gift?", ask "what does this person do every day?" Then match the gift to the habit.

The coffee or chai person

A mug is the classic for a reason: it gets used daily, it sits on their desk, and it's a quiet reminder of you. Go festive if they love Christmas, or keep it neutral so it works all year.

3. Secret Santa tips that avoid awkward moments

  • Keep it inclusive. Not everyone celebrates Christmas the same way. Winter or cosy designs work for almost anyone.
  • Skip anything too personal unless you know the person well: clothing sizes, fragrances and jokes can misfire.
  • Add a note. One handwritten line ("for your 9am meetings") makes a small gift feel thoughtful.

4. Order early

Made-to-order gifts need a few days to print and a few more to ship. For delivery before Christmas, aim to order by 12 December. Custom pet portraits need a little extra time for you to approve the preview.
