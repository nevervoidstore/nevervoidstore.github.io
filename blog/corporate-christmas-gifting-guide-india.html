Corporate Christmas Gifting in India: A Simple Planning Guide for HR Teams

4 November 2026 · 2 min read

Corporate gifting usually goes wrong in the same two ways: it starts too late, or it picks something nobody uses. This guide gives you a simple plan for both.

Start six weeks out

December is the busiest month for printers and couriers. If you want gifts on desks before the holidays, start shortlisting in early November. Starting early also gives you time to see a sample before you commit the full budget.

Set a budget per head, not a total

A per-person number makes every later decision easier: which product, whether to personalise, and whether to add packaging or a note. Check whether the budget needs to include shipping, especially if people work from different cities.

Choose gifts people keep

The best corporate gifts are used daily and seen often, so your brand or message stays visible long after December. Good options sit somewhere between useful and personal:

  • Logo mugs: simple, affordable at scale, and on desks all year.
  • Personalised mugs: each employee's own pet or name. More effort to collect details, but far more memorable.
  • Phone cases: a premium choice for leadership or top performers.

Plan how you'll collect details

Personalised gifts need input from each person: a pet photo, a name, a phone model. Collect this with a short form rather than chasing people one by one, and set a deadline a week before your order cutoff. Have a fallback design ready for anyone who doesn't reply.

Decide on delivery early

Shipping to one office is cheapest and simplest. If your team is remote or hybrid, you'll need a clean address list and a budget for individual shipping. Ask your vendor to quote both before you decide.

Always see a sample

A digital preview checks the design. A physical sample checks print quality, colour and packaging. For any order of 50 pieces or more, it's worth asking for one before paying the balance.

A week-by-week timeline

WhenWhat to do
Early NovemberSet budget per head, shortlist 2–3 gift options
Mid NovemberGet quotes, order a sample, send the details form to employees
Late NovemberApprove the sample and designs, pay the advance
1 DecemberFinal order confirmed with the vendor
Before 20 DecemberGifts delivered to the office or to each employee

Keep it inclusive

Teams in India celebrate different festivals. Framing gifts as "year-end" or "holiday" thanks, with winter or neutral designs, works for everyone.
